# HAL-161 First Pilot Client Outreach and Sales Pack

**Date:** 2026-06-25  
**Status:** Ready to use  
**Audience:** Founder/operator selling the first controlled pilot

## Goal

Turn the finished pilot-readiness work into a practical outreach pack for
finding and qualifying the first paid pilot customers.

This pack is intentionally small and direct. The target is not mass marketing;
the target is 5 qualified Bangladesh SME pilot customers who can use the current
ERP product without custom-code commitments.

## Target Customer

Best fit:

- Bangladesh SME with 5-20 employees.
- Trader, wholesaler, distributor, light service provider, or inventory-heavy
  small business.
- Currently using spreadsheets, paper, WhatsApp, or disconnected tools.
- Has one internal champion who is comfortable with web software.
- Needs sales, procurement, inventory, basic finance, CRM, or staff role control.

Not a fit yet:

- Requires custom integrations before starting.
- Requires on-site implementation under pilot pricing.
- Has no internal champion.
- Needs enterprise compliance, separate infrastructure, or strict uptime SLA.
- Wants the founder to clean and enter all historical data.

## One-Page Pilot Offer

### Halalzi ERP Early Pilot

Halalzi ERP helps small businesses move daily sales, purchasing, stock, staff
access, and basic finance out of scattered spreadsheets into one controlled
system.

### Included Modules

- Dashboard summary
- Products and stock tracking
- Sales orders and invoices
- Purchase orders and approval workflow
- Vendors
- Basic finance: receivables, payables, cash/bank, reports
- CRM: leads, deals, tasks
- Users, roles, permissions, and audit logs
- CSV exports

### Pilot Package

- Price: 5,000 BDT/month
- Duration: 3 months at pilot pricing
- Seats: up to 5 users
- Setup: 1 remote kickoff call
- Training: 1 remote training call
- Support: remote async support through WhatsApp/email
- Limit: first 5 pilot companies only

### Important Scope

- No custom software development during pilot.
- No on-site training.
- No hardware/printer/network setup.
- No manual data entry service.
- Client prepares product/vendor/customer data using provided templates.

### Success Criteria

After 30 days, the client should be able to:

- Create products, vendors, customers, sales orders, and purchase orders.
- Track basic stock movement.
- Assign users to correct roles.
- View receivable/payable status.
- Export basic operational reports.
- Reduce at least one spreadsheet-heavy workflow.

## Discovery Call Script

Duration: 20-30 minutes.

### Opening

```text
Thanks for taking the call. I will keep this practical. I want to understand how
you currently manage sales, purchase, stock, finance, and staff access, then I
will tell you honestly whether Halalzi ERP is a good pilot fit right now.
```

### Questions

1. What type of business do you run?
2. How many people are involved in daily operations?
3. What do you use now: Excel, paper, WhatsApp, Tally, QuickBooks, or something else?
4. Which workflow causes the most pain today?
5. How many sales orders or invoices do you handle monthly?
6. How many suppliers/vendors do you actively manage?
7. Do you track stock quantity today?
8. Who approves purchases?
9. Do you need separate access for sales/procurement/finance staff?
10. What report do you wish you had every week?
11. Can one person from your team act as the internal champion?
12. Can your team prepare clean product/vendor/customer data within 7 days?
13. Are you okay with remote setup and training?
14. Are you okay with no custom code during the pilot?

### Close

If fit is strong:

```text
This looks like a good pilot fit. The next step is a 45-60 minute product demo.
After that, if you want to proceed, we set up the company, users, and first data
set using a controlled onboarding checklist.
```

If fit is weak:

```text
I do not want to force a pilot if the product is not ready for your needs. Based
on what you described, the current version may not be the best fit yet. I can
keep your requirements noted and reconnect when the roadmap reaches that area.
```

## Do Not Say

Avoid these promises:

- We can customize anything you need.
- We will import all your messy data for free.
- This replaces professional accounting advice.
- This is enterprise-ready for any company size.
- We can guarantee every workflow before seeing your process.
- You can share one login across the team.

## Weekly Founder Sales Routine

Keep the routine tiny:

- Monday: Add 10 target companies.
- Tuesday: Send 10 first messages.
- Wednesday: Follow up with older prospects.
- Thursday: Run discovery/demo calls.
- Sunday: Update scorecard and decide next week focus.

Do not chase more than 5 active pilot conversations at once. The founder's
capacity is part of the product risk.

## Final Recommendation

Start with 10 warm or semi-warm prospects before broad posting. The product is
ready for qualified pilot conversations, but the sales process must protect the
same boundaries as the product: narrow scope, no custom code, remote support,
and one champion per client.
