# Pricing, Support, and Pilot Package (HAL-158)
**Date:** 2026-06-24
**Status:** Defined / Ready for Launch

## 1. Executive Summary
This document defines a realistic and sustainable pricing, support, and onboarding package for Halalzi ERP's initial pilot phase. It is specifically designed to accommodate a **solo founder** operating in the **Bangladesh SME market**, ensuring high-quality early customer experiences without risking operational burnout.

## 2. Target Customer Profile
- **Geography:** Bangladesh.
- **Size:** Small to Medium Enterprises (SMEs) with 5-20 employees.
- **Industry:** Traders, Wholesalers, Distributors, and basic Service Providers.
- **Pain Points:** Currently using spreadsheets or paper for sales, purchasing, and basic finance, struggling with data fragmentation.
- **Tech Readiness:** Must have at least one tech-savvy "champion" on their team to drive adoption.

## 3. The Pilot Offer (Early Adopter Package)
A heavily discounted, hands-on plan strictly limited to the first **5 companies** to guarantee founder bandwidth for onboarding and feedback collection.

- **Pilot Pricing:** 5,000 BDT / month (Month-to-month subscription).
- **Duration:** 3 months of pilot-tier benefits.
- **Commitment:** Client agrees to provide detailed feedback and a public testimonial / case study upon successful implementation.

### 3.1. What is Included
- **Core Modules:** CRM, Sales Orders & Invoices, Procurement (Purchase Orders), Basic Finance (AR/AP, Cash/Bank), and Product Management.
- **Seats:** Up to 5 user accounts (1 Admin + 4 Staff/Operators).
- **Hosting & Backups:** Fully managed cloud hosting with daily backups.
- **Initial Setup:** 1-hour remote kickoff call and guided setup.
- **Standard Reports:** Built-in PDF/CSV exports and dashboard metrics.

### 3.2. What is NOT Included
- **On-site / In-person Training:** All training is strictly remote (Google Meet / Zoom).
- **Data Entry / Migration Services:** We provide standard CSV templates; the client is responsible for data cleansing and entry.
- **Custom Integrations:** No custom connections to existing legacy software or niche payment gateways.
- **Hardware Setup:** No setup for local printers, barcode scanners, or networking.

## 4. Onboarding Process
To protect founder time, onboarding must be structured and repeatable:
1. **Kickoff Call (Day 1 - 1 Hour):** Remote meeting with the company champion. Define roles, setup company profile, and explain the CSV import templates for Products/Customers.
2. **Data Import (Week 1):** Client fills out CSVs; founder imports them (or guides the client to use the system).
3. **Training Call (Day 7 - 1 Hour):** Remote walkthrough of the core workflows (Quote -> Order -> Invoice -> Payment).
4. **Check-in (Day 14 - 30 Mins):** Review usage, answer questions, and address friction points.

## 5. Support Strategy & Workload Limits
As a solo founder, support must be asynchronous and strictly bounded.

- **Primary Channel (General):** Email ticketing (support@halalzi.com).
- **Secondary Channel (Pilot Only):** A dedicated WhatsApp group with the client's champion.
- **Response Times:**
  - WhatsApp: Mon-Thu, 10 AM - 6 PM BDT (Best effort, targeting < 4 hours).
  - Email: 24-48 hours.
  - Weekends (Fri-Sat): Critical system outages only.
- **Founder Workload Limit:** **Strict hard cap of 5 concurrent pilot clients.** No new sales until at least 3 of these are successfully operating self-sufficiently.

## 6. Policies & Guardrails

### 6.1. Custom Work Policy (Strict)
- **Rule:** NO custom feature development for individual pilot clients.
- **Process:** Any feature requests or workflow changes are logged in the central product roadmap. If multiple clients request the same feature, it gets prioritized for a global release. "Saying NO" is a core strategy to maintain a single unified codebase.

### 6.2. Refund & Cancellation Policy
- **Cancellation:** Month-to-month. Cancel anytime with 7 days notice before the next billing cycle.
- **Data Export:** Upon cancellation, the client can export all their data via the built-in CSV export routes.
- **Refunds:** 30-day money-back guarantee for the first month if the software does not fit their business.

### 6.3. Post-Pilot Upgrade Path
After the 3-month pilot ends:
- **Standard Pricing:** 10,000 BDT / month (or equivalent annual plan).
- **Legacy Discount:** Early adopters who provide a video testimonial and case study will be grandfathered into a permanent 20% discount (8,000 BDT / month).

## 7. Risks and Mitigations
| Risk | Mitigation |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Founder Burnout** | Bounding support strictly to business hours. Hard cap of 5 pilot clients. |
| **Feature Creep** | Enforcement of the strict "No Custom Work" policy. |
| **Failed Adoption** | Requiring an internal "champion" at the client company. 30-day check-in. |
| **Data Migration Mess** | Refusing to do manual data entry; providing strict CSV templates instead. |

## 8. Final Recommendation
**GO.**
The pricing is attractive for the local SME market, and the strict operational boundaries (5 client cap, asynchronous support, no custom code) adequately protect the solo founder from burnout. We are ready to accept our first pilot customer.
